Shuaib Khan - Registered Physiotherapist

"A hand to help you heal! Partnering with patients to break down barriers to physical function by finding the root cause of problems."

Shuaib Khan is a dedicated and experienced registered physiotherapist with PR number 0672165, practicing in Ladysmith, South Africa. With a passion for helping patients achieve their optimal physical function, Shuaib specializes in exercise-based rehabilitation using advanced techniques including dry needling and kinesiology taping.

His approach to physiotherapy is centered on identifying and addressing the root cause of physical problems rather than just treating symptoms. This comprehensive approach ensures long-lasting results and improved quality of life for his patients.

Treatment Philosophy

My approach to physiotherapy and patient care

Root Cause Analysis

Finding and addressing the underlying cause of problems rather than just treating symptoms for long-lasting results.

Patient Partnership

Working together with patients as partners in their recovery journey, empowering them to take control of their health.

Exercise-Based Approach

Focusing on therapeutic exercises and movement to restore function and prevent future injuries.

Compassionate Care

Providing empathetic and personalized care that considers the whole person, not just their condition.

Areas of Expertise

Specialized skills and treatment techniques

Manual Therapy

Advanced hands-on techniques including joint mobilization, soft tissue manipulation, and spinal adjustments to restore normal movement patterns and reduce pain.

Dry Needling

Specialized technique using fine needles to release muscle tension and trigger points, providing rapid relief from musculoskeletal pain and dysfunction.

Kinesiology Taping

Application of specialized elastic tape to support muscles and joints without restricting movement, enhancing proprioception and facilitating healing.

Exercise Rehabilitation

Customized exercise programs designed to restore strength, flexibility, and function while preventing recurrence of injuries.
