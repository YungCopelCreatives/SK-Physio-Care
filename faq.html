General Questions

Common questions about our physiotherapy practice

What conditions do you treat at SK Physio Care?

We treat a comprehensive range of conditions including:

  • Arthritic & Chronic Pain conditions
  • Pelvic Floor Dysfunction
  • Back & Neck Pain
  • Sports Injuries
  • Orthopaedic Conditions (post-surgical, fractures)
  • Respiratory Conditions (asthma, COPD, post-COVID)
  • Neurological Conditions (stroke, cerebral palsy, brain injury)
  • Joint Related Dysfunctions
  • Headaches and Migraines

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist?

No, you don't need a doctor's referral to see a physiotherapist in South Africa. You can book an appointment directly with us. However, some medical aid schemes may require a referral for reimbursement purposes.

What should I expect during my first appointment?

Your first appointment typically includes:

  1. Completing information forms about your medical history
  2. Comprehensive assessment of your condition
  3. Discussion of your goals and treatment plan
  4. Initial treatment and home exercise program

Please arrive 15 minutes early to complete paperwork and wear comfortable clothing.

How long are treatment sessions?

Initial assessments are typically 60 minutes, while follow-up sessions are usually 45 minutes. The duration may vary depending on your specific condition and treatment needs.

Treatment Questions

Questions about our treatment methods and approaches

What treatment methods do you use?

We use evidence-based treatment methods including:

  • Manual Therapy (joint mobilization, soft tissue techniques)
  • Dry Needling
  • Kinesiology Taping
  • Exercise Rehabilitation
  • Electrotherapy
  • Postural Education
  • Breathing Exercises
  • Neurological Rehabilitation Techniques

Is physiotherapy treatment painful?

While some techniques may cause mild discomfort, we always work within your pain tolerance and adjust treatment accordingly. Our goal is to reduce pain, not increase it. We'll communicate throughout your treatment to ensure your comfort.

How many treatments will I need?

The number of treatments varies depending on your condition, severity, and individual response to treatment. Some conditions may resolve in 2-4 sessions, while chronic conditions may require ongoing management. We'll provide a personalized treatment plan during your initial assessment.

What is dry needling and is it safe?

Dry needling is a technique where fine needles are inserted into trigger points to relieve muscle tension and pain. It's a safe and effective treatment when performed by trained professionals like Shuaib Khan. The needles are sterile and single-use, and the procedure is generally well-tolerated.
