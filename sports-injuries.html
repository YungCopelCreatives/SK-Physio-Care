Our Approach

We provide sport-specific rehabilitation programs designed to get athletes back to their sport safely and stronger than before.

Comprehensive Sports Injury Rehabilitation

At SK Physio Care, we specialize in treating sports injuries of all types, from acute sprains and strains to chronic overuse injuries. Our sport-specific rehabilitation approach ensures that athletes not only recover but also return to their sport with improved performance and reduced risk of re-injury.

  • Sport-specific rehabilitation protocols
  • Injury prevention strategies and education
  • Performance enhancement and conditioning
  • Return-to-sport testing and clearance

Whether you're a professional athlete, weekend warrior, or school sports participant, we tailor our rehabilitation to your specific sport and performance goals.

Our Rehabilitation Process

Step-by-step approach to sports injury recovery

1. Injury Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of the injury, including mechanism, severity, and impact on sports performance.

2. Acute Management

Pain control, inflammation reduction, and protection of the injured area to promote healing.

3. Sport-Specific Training

Progressive rehabilitation focusing on sport-specific movements and return-to-play criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about sports injury rehabilitation

What types of sports injuries do you treat?

We treat all types including sprains, strains, fractures, dislocations, tendonitis, and overuse injuries across all sports.

How long until I can return to my sport?

Return-to-sport timeline varies by injury type and severity, ranging from 2 weeks for minor strains to 6+ months for severe injuries.

Do you work with all age groups?

Yes, we treat athletes of all ages from youth sports participants to professional and recreational adult athletes.
