### **Image Style Guidelines**
- **Color Scheme:** Professional medical/healthcare imagery
- **Tone:** Clean, modern, trustworthy, caring
- **Lighting:** Bright, natural lighting preferred
- **Setting:** Clinical/professional environment
- **People:** Diverse age groups, professional attire for staff
- **Equipment:** Modern physiotherapy equipment
- **Quality:** High-resolution, professional photography

### **Technical Specifications**
- **Compression:** Optimized for web (balance quality vs file size)
- **Alt Text:** All images need descriptive alt text for accessibility
- **File Naming:** Use hyphens, lowercase letters only
- **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG for logos/graphics with transparency

### **Total Images Required: 20**
- 1 Logo
- 3 Hero carousel images
- 9 Service detail page images
- 1 About page image
- 1 Contact page image
- 5 Treatment methods images
- 1 FAQ page image
- 2 Favicon images

### **Priority Order for Creation**
1. **High Priority:** Logo, favicon, hero images
2. **Medium Priority:** Service page images, about page image
3. **Low Priority:** Treatment methods images, FAQ/contact images

### **Image Sources Recommendations**
- Professional medical stock photography
- Custom photoshoot of actual clinic and staff
- Ensure model releases for any identifiable people
- Maintain consistent style and quality across all images
