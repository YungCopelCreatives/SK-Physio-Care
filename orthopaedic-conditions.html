Comprehensive Orthopaedic Rehabilitation

At SK Physio Care, we specialize in treating a wide range of orthopaedic conditions, from post-surgical rehabilitation to conservative management of musculoskeletal disorders. Our evidence-based approach ensures optimal recovery and functional outcomes.

  • Post-surgical rehabilitation protocols
  • Fracture recovery and bone healing support
  • Joint replacement therapy and rehabilitation
  • Mobility restoration and functional improvement

We work closely with orthopaedic surgeons and other healthcare providers to ensure coordinated care for the best possible outcomes.

Our Rehabilitation Process

Step-by-step approach to orthopaedic recovery

1. Orthopaedic Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of your condition, surgical history, and functional limitations.

2. Manual Therapy

Joint mobilization, soft tissue techniques, and manual therapy to restore movement and reduce pain.

3. Functional Restoration

Progressive exercises to restore strength, mobility, and function for daily activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about orthopaedic conditions treatment

When should I start physiotherapy after surgery?

We typically begin post-surgical rehabilitation within 1-2 weeks, depending on your surgeon's protocol and your condition.

Do you treat fractures without surgery?

Yes, we provide conservative treatment for fractures that don't require surgery, focusing on proper healing and maintaining mobility.

How long does joint replacement rehab take?

Joint replacement rehabilitation typically takes 3-6 months, with most patients returning to normal activities within 3 months.
