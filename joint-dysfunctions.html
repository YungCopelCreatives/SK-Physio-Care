Our Approach

We provide comprehensive treatment for joint dysfunctions, focusing on restoring normal joint mechanics and improving overall function.

Comprehensive Joint Dysfunction Management

At SK Physio Care, we specialize in treating various joint-related dysfunctions through advanced manual therapy techniques and targeted exercise programs. Our approach focuses on restoring normal joint biomechanics, reducing pain, and improving functional movement.

  • Joint mobilization and manipulation techniques
  • Range of motion improvement programs
  • Stability exercises and muscle strengthening
  • Pain reduction and management strategies

Whether you're dealing with acute joint injuries, chronic joint stiffness, or post-surgical joint rehabilitation, we develop personalized treatment plans to address your specific condition.

Our Treatment Process

Step-by-step approach to joint rehabilitation

1. Joint Assessment

Detailed evaluation of joint mechanics, movement patterns, and functional limitations.

2. Manual Therapy

Joint mobilization, manipulation, and soft tissue techniques to restore normal movement.

3. Strengthening

Targeted exercises to support joint stability and prevent future dysfunction.

How joint physiotherapy can help you

Reduced Pain

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about joint dysfunctions treatment

What types of joint dysfunctions do you treat?

We treat various joint issues including stiffness, instability, clicking, limited range of motion, and mechanical pain in all major joints.

Is joint manipulation safe?

Yes, when performed by trained professionals. We use gentle, evidence-based techniques tailored to your condition and comfort level.

How many treatments will I need?

Treatment duration varies, but most patients see improvement within 4-8 sessions for acute issues and 8-12 for chronic conditions.
