Our Approach

We provide comprehensive treatment for back and neck pain, addressing the root causes through manual therapy, exercise, and education.

Comprehensive Spinal Pain Management

Back and neck pain are among the most common musculoskeletal complaints we treat at SK Physio Care. Our comprehensive approach addresses spinal alignment, muscle tension, posture, and underlying mechanical issues to provide lasting relief.

  • Spinal alignment and mobilization techniques
  • Muscle tension relief and soft tissue therapy
  • Posture correction and ergonomic education
  • Preventive exercises and self-management strategies

Whether you're dealing with acute injury, chronic pain, or postural issues, we develop personalized treatment plans to address your specific condition.

Our Treatment Process

Step-by-step approach to spinal rehabilitation

1. Spinal Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of spinal alignment, movement patterns, and pain triggers.

2. Manual Therapy

Spinal mobilization, joint manipulation, and soft tissue techniques to restore function.

3. Strengthening & Posture

Targeted exercises to support spinal health and prevent future episodes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about back and neck pain treatment

What causes back and neck pain?

Common causes include poor posture, muscle strain, disc problems, arthritis, and injuries. We assess each case individually.

How many treatments will I need?

Treatment duration varies, but most patients see improvement within 4-8 sessions for acute conditions and 8-12 for chronic issues.

Is spinal manipulation safe?

Yes, when performed by trained professionals. We use gentle, evidence-based techniques tailored to your condition.
