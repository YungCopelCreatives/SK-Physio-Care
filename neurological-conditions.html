Our Approach

We provide comprehensive neurological rehabilitation to improve function, independence, and quality of life for patients with neurological conditions.

Comprehensive Neurological Rehabilitation

At SK Physio Care, we specialize in treating various neurological conditions through evidence-based rehabilitation techniques. Our comprehensive approach focuses on restoring movement, improving function, and enhancing independence for patients affected by neurological disorders.

  • Stroke rehabilitation and recovery programs
  • Cerebral palsy therapy and management
  • Traumatic brain injury recovery
  • Neuromuscular re-education and training

Our treatment is tailored to each patient's specific neurological condition, focusing on maximizing functional recovery and improving quality of life.

Our Rehabilitation Process

Step-by-step approach to neurological recovery

1. Neurological Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of motor function, sensory deficits, and functional limitations.

2. Motor Re-education

Specialized techniques to retrain movement patterns and improve motor control.

3. Functional Training

Task-specific training to improve independence in daily activities.

Benefits of Our Treatment

How neurological physiotherapy can help you

Improved Movement

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about neurological conditions treatment

What neurological conditions do you treat?

We treat stroke, cerebral palsy, traumatic brain injury, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, and other neurological disorders.

How soon after a stroke should therapy begin?

Early rehabilitation is crucial. We typically begin within days to weeks after the stroke, depending on medical stability.

Can neurological conditions be completely cured?

While some conditions may not be completely curable, significant functional improvement and quality of life enhancement are always possible.
