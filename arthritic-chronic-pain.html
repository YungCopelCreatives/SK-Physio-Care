Comprehensive Arthritis & Chronic Pain Management

At SK Physio Care, we specialize in treating various forms of arthritis and chronic pain conditions through evidence-based physiotherapy interventions. Our approach focuses on reducing pain, improving joint mobility, and enhancing your overall quality of life.

  • Pain management techniques and education
  • Joint mobility and flexibility improvement
  • Strength building and functional exercises
  • Lifestyle modifications and self-management strategies

Our treatment plans are tailored to your specific condition, whether you're dealing with osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, fibromyalgia, or other chronic pain conditions.

Our Treatment Process

Step-by-step approach to your recovery

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about arthritis and chronic pain treatment

What types of arthritis do you treat?

We treat various types including osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, and other inflammatory conditions.

How long does treatment take to show results?

Most patients experience improvement within 4-6 weeks, but this varies depending on the severity and type of condition.

Is the treatment painful?

While some techniques may cause mild discomfort, we always work within your pain tolerance and adjust treatment accordingly.
